The RX webapp

This is where the mapping happens. The RX webapp at rx.mesh-hunter.eu pairs with a MeshCore companion radio over Web Bluetooth and logs every packet that radio hears, timestamped and placed with your phone's GPS. Walk or drive, and coverage appears behind you.

It installs as a PWA: open it on your phone and add it to your home screen. Your account is made here too, from the companion you paired, and it is what puts your own receptions on the map in full detail.

The map

The map at map.mesh-hunter.eu is where every mapper's receptions meet: all of them, on one coverage map, so you can see what a node reaches beyond your own radio. It is also the hunting surface, where you follow one node's signal to where it transmits from.

Reading it takes no account. Log in with the one you made in the RX webapp to see your own receptions in full detail.

Get an account

The map reads fine without one: all-time coverage, coarse positions, and the last 24 hours of individual receptions. An account sharpens that, and it starts with a radio rather than with a sign-up form, which is why it is made in the RX webapp.

  1. Pair your MeshCore companion to the RX webapp over Bluetooth and start mapping.
  2. Register there. The account is made from the companion you paired, which is why the map itself has no sign-up form.
  3. Log in with it on the map. Your own receptions now show in full detail, everyone else's stay coarse and recent.
  4. Ask an admin for member verification. That opens the full history, Locate and the CoreScope layers for everyone's data, not only your own.

About & access

Mesh-Hunter is run by amateur-radio operators. Coverage is inferred from radio measurements via mesh topology, not from GPS tracking of any target node.

Verification to a member account (full map access) is granted by the admins. See Get an account for the steps.
