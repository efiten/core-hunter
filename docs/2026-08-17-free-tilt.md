# Free tilt by gesture, and why the 2026-07-11 "no new gesture" decision is reversed

Decision for #333. Written 2026-08-17 against `app/src/huntmap.js` as it stands,
and measured against the bundled MapLibre 4.7.1 in the running app.

## What is being reversed

`docs/2026-07-11-3d-mode.md` states:

> No new gesture: the FAB is the only way to pitch the camera (`pitchWithRotate`
> stays `false`, matching the existing rotate-gesture behaviour from #116) — kept
> in scope with the original decision ("just toggles pitch"), not a free-tilt
> 3D explorer.

That decision stands for what it was scoped to: 3D mode as a FAB toggle between
two fixed pitches. #333 changes the goal. A near-horizontal camera reads as
looking *along* the terrain toward a target rather than down at it, and there is
no fixed second angle that serves both "give me some relief" and "let me sight
down the ridge". The angle has to be continuous, which means a gesture.

That doc is left as written. This entry is the later decision, not an edit to
the earlier one.

## The two gesture paths are not one setting

Measured, not assumed:

- **Touch pitch was already enabled.** `touchPitch` is not passed, and it
  defaults on in 4.7.1. Two-finger tilt worked on device before #333 and was
  merely clamped.
- **`pitchWithRotate` governs the mouse path only** (ctrl/right-drag). Left
  `false`, a desktop browser has no tilt gesture whatsoever.
- **MapLibre's default `maxPitch` is 60**, the same angle the view FAB eases to
  (`PITCH_3D`, `maplayers.js`). So the touch gesture bottomed out exactly where
  the FAB left off.

Measured with the app's own option object:

| `maxPitch` | `getMaxPitch()` | `setPitch(90)` |
|---|---|---|
| not passed | 60 | clamps to 60 |
| 85 | 85 | clamps to 85 |
| 86 | throws `maxPitch must be less than or equal to 85` | |

85 is the library's hard maximum. 90 is not offered because a camera level with
the horizon projects to infinity.

## Decision

1. `maxPitch: 85`. Lifts the ceiling for the gesture that already existed.
2. `pitchWithRotate: true`. Gives the mouse path a tilt gesture, so the raised
   ceiling is reachable on a laptop and not only on a phone.
3. `PITCH_3D` stays 60. The FAB remains the introduction to 3D; the gesture
   takes you the rest of the way. The FAB is still a fixed-angle toggle, so the
   #258 five-state cycle is unaffected.

The prototype behind #335 ran with all three, which is the configuration the
on-device evaluation was done against.

## What this makes reachable, and the open cost

Above roughly 70 degrees a large part of the viewport sits above the horizon,
where MapLibre draws nothing without a sky layer. It reads as a broken map. That
is #397, and it is the reason this is a real dependency rather than a polish
item: before #333 the camera could not exceed 60, where no void exists.

Also known, and not fixed here:

- #318 (hex-3d/points-3d z-fighting against buildings-3d) reads worse at 85
  than at 60, because extreme pitch makes overlapping extrusions far more
  visible.
- A near-horizontal camera sees much further, so it pulls in more tiles per
  frame. The terrain spike measured 16 DEM tiles at pitch 85 against roughly 1
  top-down; that figure is for a DEM source the app does not have, but the
  direction applies to basemap and building tiles too.
